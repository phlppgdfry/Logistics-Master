# Logistics Master

**A portfolio of operational data products, simulations and case studies for ports and supply chains.**

This repository turns logistics problems—yard capacity, ETA uncertainty, terminal delays, empty mileage and operational visibility—into working prototypes, data models and decision-support tools. It is a portfolio and learning repository: each item clearly represents either a runnable prototype, a case study or research.

> Looking for a quick tour? Start with the five projects below. For the scope of company-specific material, read the [public-source and independence notice](./DISCLAIMER.md).

## What this portfolio shows

- **Logistics domain thinking** — terminal, warehouse, transport and supply-chain workflows translated into measurable operational problems.
- **Engineering** — APIs, dashboards, data pipelines, simulations, tests and deployable prototypes.
- **Data and analysis** — KPI models, structured datasets, validation workflows and decision-support views.
- **Product and business analysis** — user needs, process design, ROI framing and implementation trade-offs.

## Project maturity

This is a deliberately mixed portfolio. Individual folders may be one of the following:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Runnable prototype** | A working technical demonstration; not presented as a production deployment. |
| **Case study** | A structured analysis of a realistic business or operational problem. |
| **Research** | Public-source desk research and technology mapping. |
| **Concept** | An illustrative product or architecture direction, intended to prompt discussion. |
